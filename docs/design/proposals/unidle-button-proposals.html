Unidle · button proposals

Four ways to press "stay awake"

Each window below is live: click the button to switch it on. All four share the same premium finish. Decision: all four ship as user-selectable styles, with Halo (A) as the default.

Deeper neutrals

Graphite tinted slightly green instead of flat grey, so the accent feels native. Dark mode becomes the hero theme.

Light and depth

Soft top-left highlight, inner shadows and one restrained glow when on. No flat fills on the main control.

Geist type

Geist and Geist Mono (open-source, OFL), bundled with the app so every OS looks the same. Mono for the timer.

Bigger control

The button grows from 88 × 48 to roughly 170–240 px. It becomes the window, not a widget in it.

Halo

A large tactile power disc. Off it sits matte and quiet. On it fills with a green gradient, breathes slowly and sends out soft ripple rings.

  • Universal power symbol, instantly understood
  • Calmest option, closest to a hardware button

Orbit

A ring around the button. When on, a glowing comet circles it, reading as "something is moving your mouse". The elapsed time moves into the button itself.

  • Motion literally shows activity being simulated
  • Ring can later show schedule progress (time left today)

Pulse switch

Keeps today's switch shape, at 236 × 104 with a heavy machined knob that springs across. A heartbeat line traces through the track while on.

  • Clearest on/off affordance (it is a switch)
  • "Alive" heartbeat metaphor, no new concepts

Wake eye

The most "Unidle" option. Off, a closed eye snoozes with drifting z's. On, the squircle rounds into a disc, the eye opens, glances around and blinks now and then.

  • Doubles as the app icon and tray icon
  • Most personality; works best if the brand stays playful
