# Code of conduct

This project is a welcoming place for everyone, regardless of background or experience.

**Expected:** be respectful and patient, assume good intent, give feedback on the work and not the person, and accept correction gracefully.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sharing someone's private information, or any conduct that makes others feel unsafe.

**Reporting:** contact the maintainer through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/ThroughTheWind). Reports are handled privately. Maintainers may remove content or block participants who break these rules.
